Chapter I

The Comment

In March 2026, Timothée Chalamet sat down with Matthew McConaughey for a Variety and CNN town hall, and said something that managed to offend an entire sector of the arts world in about fifteen words.

The Comment

"I don't want to be working in things where it's like, 'Hey, keep this thing alive, even though like no one cares about this anymore.' All respect to all the ballet and opera people out there." — Timothée Chalamet, March 2026

To be fair, Chalamet wasn't trying to take down ballet. He was making a point that he wants to work in something people want to do, not something people feel obligated to save, which is reasonable. The problem is the example he picked. Ballet and opera have been filling houses for centuries. But as always, the internet took this comment and RAN.

The Irony Chalamet has previously spoken about his family's connection to the New York City Ballet, the same institution he implicitly dismissed.

Chapter III

Ebbing and Flowing

The notion that ballet and opera are "dying art forms" confuses transformation for decline. Ballet is not just Swan Lake and the Nutcracker. These cherished forms have always absorbed new influences as the times have changed. This takes form in new choreographers, composers, and intended audiences. What looks like irrelevance from the outside is ACTUALLY an institution doing something different than it did fifty years ago.

2006
The Met launched its Live in HD cinema series in December 2006 with a transmission of Mozart's The Magic Flute, which was the first time any arts institution had attempted live satellite broadcasts to movie theaters. The series has since sold more than 33 million tickets and now reaches over 70 countries.
2022/2023
#Balletcore goes viral! Ballet aesthetics take over TikTok, accumulating over 1.4 billion views. This sparked a surge in young adults enrolling in ballet classes, with "adult ballet" searched over 34.7 million times on TikTok alone.
2023/2024
The Royal Ballet and Opera has a sold-out season and youngest audience on record this season drew over one million visitors to Covent Garden. Young people became the single largest audience group.
2023/2024
Ballet becomes mainstream fashion when Reformation launches a collection with the New York City Ballet, shot at Lincoln Center. The same year, AllSaints partners with The Royal Ballet on a streetwear collection featuring active dancers. Now we begin to see ballet walking down the street.

Chapter IV

The Deeper Problem

Why did Chalamet say this so easily, as if ballet or opera are the obvious choices for dying art forms? The main difference here is media. Presence on television, Instagram, Twitter, Netflix is what boosts ratings of sports and cinema, so for a lot of people, ballet and opera's presences don't register in their daily lives.

"Relevance" is often a proxy for visibility. What we can't stream, we assume is dying.

One this we have to note is this is not the same and simply not existing at all. Art forms that exist in physical spaces bring us together in ways that screens just can't. Involving the audience in a private expereince touches a different part of us than the ones that we see on the For You Page. Why should we be okay with assuming that just because something isn't online means it's dying? We have to examine this further.

Chapter V

The Arts World Responds

The institutions Chalamet dismissed didn't exactly go quiet about it.

Their influence can be felt across theatre, film, contemporary music, fashion, and beyond. For centuries, these disciplines have shaped the way artists create and audiences experience culture, and today millions of people around the world continue to enjoy and engage with them."
Source: The Hollywood Reporter, March 6, 2026
The Royal Ballet and Opera (Instagram)

Conclusion

Alive and Well,
Thank you very much.

Examples matter. Chalamet isn't wrong to care about cinema. After all, he is an incredible actor. But the example he chose to make that point didn't hold up.

Ballet and opera aren't always going to be trending topics like cheating scandals and podcast host beef. However, they are filling seats, training new generations of incredibly dedicated performers and actively reinventing what they look like as the world evolves. We ourselves have to be intellectually curious and seek out these important art forms for ourselves.

Ballet and Opera

are not in their final act.
